dmg_boot.bin is the bundled Bootix DMG v1.2 open-source replacement (CC0-1.0). The same firmware is embedded in ReaBoy.exe. A valid custom DMG (rom\dmg_boot.bin) or CGB (rom\cgb_boot.bin) firmware selected through File > Choose Boot ROM takes priority. Put your own .gb or .gbc games here and choose File > Open ROM. No commercial games or proprietary Nintendo firmware dumps are supplied.
